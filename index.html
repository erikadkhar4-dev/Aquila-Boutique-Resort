Welcome to Aquila

Beachfront living, built from wood and salt air

Tucked beside the tree line at Agonda Beach, Aquila's cabins are raised on stilts and roofed in coconut thatch and corrugated tin, facing the Arabian Sea straight on. Every balcony looks at the same tide.

By day it's a quiet stretch of sand with sunbeds and shade; by night the bistro strings its lanterns and the kitchen turns out fresh catch, seafood risotto, and cold beer until the last table leaves.

No Limits Bistro @ Aquila

Fresh catch, cold drinks, sand underfoot

The bar sits open to the beach, thatch overhead and lanterns strung low. Regulars point to the fresh fish and seafood risotto — the kitchen leans coastal Goan with a full bar alongside.
